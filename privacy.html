Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Number Hub (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the Number Hub mobile application (“App”). It covers the App's local game-progress storage and its use of Google AdMob to display advertisements.

1. About Number Hub

Number Hub is a collection of number-based puzzle games. The App does not require you to create an account or sign in to play. Based on the current app setup described for this policy, game scores, settings, and progress are stored locally on your device rather than in a Number Hub account or our own game-progress server.

2. Information stored by the App

Game progress stored on your device: The App may store gameplay information such as scores, best scores, progress, and game settings in local storage on your device so that your game experience can continue between sessions. This information is not uploaded by the App to a Number Hub account or cloud game profile.

Information you send to support: If you contact us, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, device details, and a description of your question or problem. We use this information to respond to you and provide support.

We do not operate user registration, login, or account profiles in the current version of the App. We do not use a separate analytics service in the current setup described for this policy.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information from your device to deliver ads, measure advertising performance, help prevent fraud, and maintain the advertising service.

Depending on the SDK version, configuration, your device settings, and your choices, this information may include:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers.
  • Interactions with the App and advertisements, such as app launches, taps, and video ad views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, stability, and SDK performance.
  • Information needed to select, deliver, limit, and measure advertisements.

Google may use information processed through AdMob for advertising, analytics related to advertising, fraud prevention, and service operation. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. The Google Mobile Ads SDK states that data it collects is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

4. Advertising choices

You may be able to reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or manage advertising privacy settings, through your device's Android settings. The advertisements you see may depend on your location, applicable consent requirements, device settings, and Google's advertising controls. Where required, Google consent tools may be presented to ask for your choices about ads and the use of information.

Changing or deleting an advertising ID does not necessarily stop all advertising or all data processing by the advertising SDK.

5. How information is used

We use locally stored gameplay information to provide basic game functionality and preserve your progress on your device. We use information you send to support to respond to your request. Google AdMob processes information as described above to provide and maintain advertising services. We do not use a separate analytics product in the current app setup described for this policy.

6. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Gameplay information kept only in the App's local storage is not shared by us with a game-progress server. Information may be processed or shared with Google through the AdMob SDK for advertising and related purposes described in Section 3. If you contact support, your message is handled by the email or support provider you use to send it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by the App, you clear the App's storage or data, or you uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may permanently remove locally saved scores and progress.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and manage the request, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with legal obligations. Google determines retention for information processed through AdMob under its own policies and settings.

8. Children's privacy

Number Hub is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not provide user accounts. Advertising content and data handling may be affected by the app's target-audience settings, applicable law, and the configuration of AdMob. If you intend to make the App available to children or include children in its target audience, you must configure AdMob and the App to meet all applicable child-protection, families, consent, and advertising requirements before release.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support message, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the App's operation to protect information we handle. Gameplay information is stored locally on your device and its security may depend on your device's own security settings. Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing. For information processed by Google through AdMob, you may also use Google's privacy controls and the controls available on your device. Contact us using the details in Section 12 for requests relating to information we handle directly.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our services, legal requirements, or advertising integrations change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and change the effective date above. Please review this page periodically.
